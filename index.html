About

Senior WordPress Developer, 4.5+ years

Senior WordPress Developer based in Nashik with 4.5+ years of hands-on experience developing and designing websites. Well-versed in Agile methodologies and coding languages. Dedicated to developing creative solutions that meet business goals and requirements.

Day to day, that means custom WordPress themes and plugins, ACF / ACF Pro, Gutenberg and custom blocks, WooCommerce, REST APIs, and headless WordPress — alongside JavaScript, React, Angular, HTML/CSS, Tailwind, and Bootstrap. I also work with third-party API, CRM, Zapier, Make, and HubSpot integrations when a site needs to connect to the rest of the stack.
